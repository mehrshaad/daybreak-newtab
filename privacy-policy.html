Privacy Policy

Daybreak — New Tab · Last updated: July 21, 2026

Daybreak ("the extension") is a browser extension that replaces the Chrome new tab page with a customizable start page. This policy explains what data the extension accesses and how it is handled. In short: the extension does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information to the developer. It has no analytics, no advertising, no user accounts, and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

All of your preferences — the selected wallpaper, your shortcuts, your to-do items, and your list of cities — are saved locally in your browser using localStorage. This information stays on your device, is never sent to the developer, and is removed if you uninstall the extension or clear your browser data.

Information sent to third parties

To provide certain features, the extension communicates directly with the following third-party services. The developer does not operate any server and does not receive any of this data.

  • Weather — OpenWeatherMap. When the weather feature is enabled, the name(s) of the city/cities you have configured are sent to OpenWeatherMap (api.openweathermap.org) to retrieve the current weather. Their handling of that request is governed by the OpenWeatherMap Privacy Policy.
  • Search — Google. If you type a query into the search box and submit it, that query is sent to Google Search to display results, exactly as it would be on Google's own website. This is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Any other links in the extension (shortcuts and the Google apps menu) simply open the website you click, the same as any ordinary bookmark or link.

Permissions

The extension does not request any special Chrome permissions. It only overrides the new tab page.

Data sale and sharing

The developer does not collect, sell, rent, or share any user data with anyone. The extension is not used for any purpose unrelated to its single purpose of providing a customizable new tab page.

Children's privacy

The extension is a general-purpose new tab page and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the project's GitHub repository: github.com/mehrshaad/daybreak-newtab (open an issue).
